# In-person event policy \- OLS

Attending in-person events: OLS staff and contractors attend in-person events when invited, or when an event is deemed of relevance to OLS’s mission. This policy is primarily focused on *organising* events.

**TL;DR**: OLS doesn’t independently organise in-person events. We may consider collaborating with others who are taking the lead.

## OLS is online, and each community and organisation is different

OLS is, by nature, a primarily remote organisation, with staff, contractors, and community spread across the globe and connected synchronously and asynchronously via the internet. We recognise that what we say here may not be true for all organisations.

## For OLS, in-person is for deepening relationships and building trust

OLS core staff and contractors try to attend a joint in-person retreat every year if possible. We value the time to connect \- not just in sporadic work sessions and meetings, but also in casual leisure activities, cooking, crafting, and silly jokes whilst planning together for the future. Whilst it is impossible to *force* connection and psychological safety, it is possible to design scenarios which make it more likely to happen between team members.

## OLS generally won’t organise larger-scale in-person events on its own

We are not against them by any means, but we would rather focus on small-scale (e.g. team) events and/or online. Tooting our own horn, perhaps \- but we think we do online really well.

## Collaborating with others: Always happy to consider\!

If others are organising something in-person, we’ll be happy to chat about ways to work together.

### Past collaborations

In the past, for example, we have:

- Organised informal meet-ups at events where there are several community members attending already, e.g. “come over to our airbnb and we’ll eat takeaway together”  
- Collaborated with DRA on OSPARK, which has both online and in-person aspects  
- Fiscally hosted the UK Open Science Retreat 2026\.

### Types of collaboration we would consider

- Accessibility / EDI collaborations  
  - Advisory services that take into consideration the available budget, e.g. from volunteer-only and up to well-funded conferences.  
  - Microgrants / bursaries (See the kinds of things we offer for microgrants).  
  - Better quality **hybrid** participation, e.g. providing OWL, lapel mic, and streaming for in-person, with human-corrected transcripts and recordings afterwards.  
- Co-organising:  
  - Small satellite events attached to a larger event.  
  - Online-only “repeat” events of in-person events.  
- Other? This is not an exhaustive list.
